# Elmer magnetic-motion study — withdrawn

**Status: withdrawn from engineering use.** The published Elmer images, GIF and related exploratory inputs did not model the coil and magnetic geometry correctly for the active nine-coil motor. They are retained for traceability only and do not support force, flux, heating or motion conclusions.

The active dimensions are in [docs/11-current-build.md](docs/11-current-build.md). The audit and requirements for a replacement correlated model are in [docs/13-project-audit.md](docs/13-project-audit.md).
