# Current nine-coil layout

`current-nine-coil-layout.svg` is a dimensional **schematic**, not a finite-element field plot or an animation. It depicts the active 39-section, 466 mm magnet rod, 12 mm pitch, nine 5 mm coils at 8 mm centres and the rail-side RLS encoder.

## Withdrawn visual material

- `rev64_real_life_motion.gif` and `rev64_animation_preview.png` show the recovered Rev64 six-coil concept at a 40 mm stroke. They are historical visuals only and do not represent the current motor.
- `elmer_coil_motion_study.gif` and its rendered frames are withdrawn from engineering use. The Elmer geometry did not represent the annular coil pack or its field correctly.

These files remain in the repository for traceability. Do not use them to estimate force, flux, movement, fit, or thermal behaviour.
